Hello! I'm Minbok Wi.

I am a Staff Engineer in the AP Architecture Group (MX Division) at Samsung Electronics. As
a performance architect, I model and develop the subsystems of mobile Application
Processors (SoCs): driving architecture definition, analyzing workload performance to
remove system-level bottlenecks, and maximizing power efficiency for next-generation
mobile platforms.

I received my Ph.D. from Seoul National University, where I was advised by
[Prof. Jung Ho Ahn](https://geniajh.github.io/). During my doctoral studies, I spent time
as a Visiting Scholar at UT Austin, collaborating with
[Prof. Mattan Erez](https://lph.ece.utexas.edu/merez/MattanErez/Home). My doctoral research
centered on the security, reliability, and characterization of DRAM-based memory systems.
