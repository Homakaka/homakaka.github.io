About

Hello! I’m Minbok Wi.

I am a Staff Engineer in the AP Architecture Group (MX Division) at Samsung Electronics. As a performance architect, I model and develop the subsystems of mobile Application Processors (SoCs): driving architecture definition, analyzing workload performance to remove system-level bottlenecks, and maximizing power efficiency for next-generation mobile platforms.

I received my Ph.D. from Seoul National University, where I was advised by Prof. Jung Ho Ahn. During my doctoral studies, I spent time as a Visiting Scholar at UT Austin, collaborating with Prof. Mattan Erez. My doctoral research centered on the security, reliability, and characterization of DRAM-based memory systems.

Research

My work spans computer architecture, memory architecture & systems, and hardware security & reliability.

  • Computer architecture: architectural modeling and performance optimization of processor and system-on-chip subsystems across CPU, interconnect, and memory; system-level bottleneck analysis and power-efficiency trade-offs. In my current role at Samsung, this focuses on mobile Application Processors (SoCs).
  • Memory architecture & systems: DRAM microarchitecture, address mapping, and timing behavior; memory-subsystem performance; emerging memory interfaces such as CXL.
  • Hardware security & reliability: RowHammer and DRAM disturbance attacks and their mitigations; DRAM reverse-engineering through direct command issuing; reliable and secure memory design (on-die ECC, CXL memory).
